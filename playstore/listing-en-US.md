## Full description
Mon Budget Familial is a simple family budgeting app built around one practical question: how much can I safely spend after my bills, savings and financial goals are protected?

The app helps you organize your money without turning budgeting into a complicated accounting exercise.

Main features:
- track received and expected income;
- plan mandatory bills and due dates;
- create budget envelopes for everyday spending;
- record expenses quickly;
- protect savings before spending;
- prepare for future large expenses with provisions;
- track financial goals;
- see your Safe to spend amount at a glance;
- follow a recommended daily budget;
- review your financial discipline score;
- export and back up your data;
- use the app offline;
- enable cloud synchronization when you choose.

Mon Budget Familial supports multiple currencies and does not perform foreign-exchange conversion. Your selected currency changes how your budget is displayed; existing amounts are never silently converted.

Mon Budget Familial is a budgeting and personal finance organization tool. It does not lend money, transfer money, hold funds, or provide personalized professional financial advice.
