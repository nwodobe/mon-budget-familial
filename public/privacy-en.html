Privacy Policy — Mon Budget Familial

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Mon Budget Familial is a personal and family budgeting application. It helps users record income, bills, expenses, savings, budget envelopes, goals and provisions in order to manage their budget.

Data processed

The application may process information voluntarily entered by the user: email address when creating a cloud account, household and member names, income, expenses, bills, savings, budgets, goals, provisions and usage preferences.

Local mode and cloud backup

The application can work without an account. In that case, budget data remains stored locally on the device. When a user chooses cloud backup and signs in, their data is synchronized with the Supabase infrastructure configured for Mon Budget Familial.

Purposes

Data is used only to provide requested features: calculating available budget, expense tracking, synchronization, data restoration, reports and account security features. It is not sold to advertisers.

Security and access

Cloud data is isolated by user through database-level security policies. The client application uses only a public key intended for client applications and does not embed any database administration key.

Sharing and service providers

Cloud backup and authentication rely on Supabase. Google Play may process information related to downloads, distribution and, when subscriptions are enabled, purchases made through Google Play Billing according to its own policies.

Retention

Local data remains on the device until it is deleted, the application is reset, or it is removed according to the operating system's behavior. Cloud data remains associated with the account until that account is deleted.

Account and data deletion

A signed-in user can request permanent deletion of their account from the application. Deleting the cloud account deletes the cloud data linked to it. Local data on a device can also be erased from the application settings.

An external procedure is also available on the Delete my account page.

Children

Mon Budget Familial is not designed as a service specifically intended for children. Parents or legal guardians should supervise any use by a minor.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when features, service providers or regulatory obligations change. The update date is shown at the top of this page.
