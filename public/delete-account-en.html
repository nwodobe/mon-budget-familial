Delete my Mon Budget Familial account

This page explains how to delete your Mon Budget Familial cloud account and the cloud data associated with it.

From the application:
  1. Open More.
  2. Go to Account, then Delete my account.
  3. Check the signed-in account.
  4. Confirm permanent deletion.

Data deleted

Deleting the account removes the sign-in identity and cloud data linked to that account, including household settings, income, bills, expenses, envelopes, savings movements, goals, provisions and other synchronized budget data.

Local data

Data stored only on a device can be erased separately from the sensitive settings area of the application. After account deletion, the application may also erase local data on the device used to confirm the request.

Warning: account deletion is permanent. Export a backup before confirming if you want to keep a copy of your data.
