# Google Play products

These identifiers are part of the application/server contract and must be created exactly as written in Play Console:

| Product ID | Type | Commercial target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `premium_monthly` | Auto-renewing subscription | 1,500 FCFA / month |
| `premium_annual` | Auto-renewing subscription | 12,000 FCFA / year |

Recommended launch offer: 14-day free trial, configured as a Play Console subscription offer rather than hard-coded in the application.

Prices shown in the Android app are loaded from Google Play ProductDetails when available, so Google Play remains the payment/pricing source of truth for each market.
